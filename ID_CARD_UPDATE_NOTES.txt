ID CARD UPDATE — TEACHER DETAILS

Run db/schema_id_card_verification.sql in Supabase after installing this update.

This migration:
- adds teachers.sex and teachers.phone if they do not already exist;
- keeps the existing student/teacher QR tokens;
- changes public ID-card verification to show Form Master, Sex and Phone;
- removes Classes Taught and Subjects Taught from ID-card verification;
- keeps session/term out of the physical ID cards.

The Teachers admin form now lets the school enter/edit Sex and Phone.
